BCML Lab, Heriot-Watt University

Supervised by Prof. Wei Pang

I am a PhD researcher in the BCML Lab, School of Mathematical and Computer Sciences at Heriot-Watt University, under the supervision of Prof. Wei Pang. My research interests focus on agentic AI, multi-agent systems, and the development of AI-driven solutions for biomedical applications and real-world industrial challenges.

My academic and professional background combines artificial intelligence, aerospace engineering, and industrial systems. I obtained an MSc in Aerospace Dynamics from Cranfield University in 2021, following a First Class Honours degree in Mechanical Engineering from De Montfort University. Since 2022, I have been working with Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China (COMAC), where I am responsible for managing airframe and system suppliers for the C919 and ARJ21 (C909) aircraft programmes. Through this role, I have gained experience in large-scale engineering systems, supplier coordination, and complex industrial project management, while maintaining a strong interest in bridging advanced AI research with practical deployment in high-impact domains.
